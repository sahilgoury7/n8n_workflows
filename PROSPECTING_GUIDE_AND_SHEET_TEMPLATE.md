# 🎯 Real Estate Prospecting & Lead Sourcing Blueprint
## Kaunse Channel Partners (CPs) aur Builders Dhoondhne Hain, Kahan se aur Kaise Track Karne Hain

> **Target:** 30–50 High-Intent Real Estate Channel Partners & Local Builders  
> **Ideal Client Profile (ICP):** Mid-sized property brokers / CPs & local builders (active 2BHK/3BHK/Plot inventory, Average ticket size > ₹40 Lakhs - ₹1.5 Cr)  
> **Primary Goal:** Unhe find karna jo **already Facebook/Instagram par Property Ads chala rahe hain** aur delayed reply ki wajah se buyer enquiries kho rahe hain.

---

## 🔍 1. Lead mein Kya-Kya Dekhna Hai? (Green Flags vs Red Flags)

Har kisi property dealer ko call karke time waste nahi karna. Sirf unhi ko target karo jahan deal close hone ke 90% chances hon:

### 🟢 Green Flags (Inhi ko Target Karo):
1. **Active Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Ads:**
   - *Kyun:* Jo CP ya builder ad chala raha hai, wo marketing par daily ₹2,000 se ₹10,000 kharch kar raha hai. Unke paas budget bhi hai aur buyer leads drop hone ka asli dard bhi hai!
2. **Ticket Size > ₹40 Lakh – ₹1.5 Cr (Average Brokerage ₹80,000 – ₹2,50,000 per deal):**
   - *Kyun:* Agar ek flat bikne par CP ko ₹1 Lakh brokerage milti hai, toh aapka ₹18,000 ka system sirf **ek extra sale** bachane par 5x ROI de deta hai!
3. **Sweet-Spot Size (1 se 3 Offices, 5 se 15 Telecallers):**
   - *Kyun:* DLF, Godrej, ya Lodha jaise giant corporate builders ko mat pakdo (unka decision 6 mahine corporate committee mein hota hai). Local Channel Partners ya mid-size builders ko pakdo jahan **Owner / Director seedha office me baithta hai aur 1 din me advance payment transfer karta hai!**
4. **Ad par WhatsApp CTA Button ("Send WhatsApp Message"):**
   - Direct proof ki wo WhatsApp par leads bula rahe hain.

### 🔴 Red Flags (Inhe Skip Karo):
- ❌ National Mega Developers (Godrej Properties, DLF, Prestige) — decision maker tak nahi pahuch paoge.
- ❌ Chhote single-agent brokers jo sirf OLX ya 99acres par free listing daalte hain aur ads nahi chalate.
- ❌ Rental-only agents (PG/Room rent brokers jinka ticket size ₹1,000 - ₹2,000 hota hai).

## 🚀 2. Leads Kahan se aur Kaise Nikaalni Hain? (Top 3 Free Methods)

---

### Method A: Meta Ads Library (Sabse Powerful — 80% Leads Yahan Milengi)

Meta Ads Library par har us real estate agency/builder ki list mil jaati hai jo abhi live ad chala raha hai:

1. Browser mein open karo: [https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/)
2. Filters set karo:
   - **Country:** `India`
   - **Ad Category:** `All Ads`
3. Search bar mein keywords daalo:
   - `"2 BHK flat ready to move"`
   - `"3 BHK luxury apartment"`
   - `"Commercial shops for sale"`
   - `"Gated community villa"`
   - `"Residential plots near airport"`
4. **City-Specific Search (Highest Conversion):**
   - Search: `"2 BHK flat Noida"`, `"Luxury flats Gurgaon"`, `"Flats in Hinjewadi Pune"`, `"Property in Bangalore"`, `"Flats in Whitefield"`, `"Plots in Lucknow"`, `"Villas in Jaipur"`.
5. **Ad ko Inspect karo:**
   - Ad par click karo: Dekho kya ad par **"Send WhatsApp Message"** button laga hai?
   - Agar haan, toh link open karke unka official WhatsApp business number copy kar lo!
   - Unka Facebook page / Instagram handle open karke agency ka naam aur owner ka naam note kar lo.

---

### Method B: Google Maps (High-End Real Estate Hubs)

Aapke target city ke prime property hubs search karo:
- **Noida / Greater Noida:** Sector 62, Sector 150, Noida Expressway
- **Gurgaon:** Golf Course Extension, Sohna Road, Dwarka Expressway
- **Pune:** Baner, Wakad, Hinjewadi, Kharadi
- **Bangalore:** Whitefield, Sarjapur, Electronic City, Hebbal
- **Mumbai / Thane:** Thane West, Navi Mumbai, Malad

**Steps:**
1. Google Maps par search karo: `"Real estate channel partner in Baner Pune"` ya `"Property consultant in Sector 62 Noida"`.
2. List mein un firms par click karo jinke **20 se 150 reviews** hain (ye active mid-sized firms hain).
3. Unki website ya Google My Business profile se owner/manager ka number copy karo.

---

### Method C: LinkedIn se Owner / Director ka Naam Dhoondhna

Jab aapko agency ka naam mil jaye (jaise *"Prime Horizon Real Estate"*):
1. LinkedIn par search karo: `Prime Horizon Real Estate Director` ya `Founder` ya `Managing Partner`.
2. Owner ka exact naam mil jayega (e.g. *Rohit Mehta*).
3. **Kyun zaroori hai:** Jab cold call karoge, toh receptionist se bolo:  
   > *"Hello, mujhe Rohit Sir se unke active 2BHK Meta ads ke regarding baat karni hai."*  
   Receptionist turant call Owner ko transfer kar degi!

- **Day 2:** Cold Calling Sprint (Daily 20-25 calls: 11:00 AM – 1:30 PM & 3:30 PM – 5:30 PM) ➔ **3-4 Live Demos Book karein**.
