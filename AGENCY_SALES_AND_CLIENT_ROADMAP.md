## 🏢 3.5 Multi-Branch / Multi-Location Lead Routing (2-3 Branches)

Agar kisi coaching institute ki **2 ya 3 alag-alag branches** hain (e.g. *Branch 1: Kalu Sarai, Branch 2: Janakpuri, Branch 3: Noida*), toh unke liye lead qualify aur alert bhejne ka system aur bhi zyada powerful ban jata hai (aur aap iske **₹40,000 – ₹50,000** charge kar sakte ho!):

### Ye n8n mein Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

1. **AI Naturally Branch Poochta Hai:**
   - Conversation ke dauran AI Agent student se naturally poochta hai:  
     *"Aap hamare kaunse centre par offline batch ya demo attend karna chahenge — Kalu Sarai, Janakpuri ya Noida?"*
   - AI Output Parser mein ek field add ho jata hai: `preferred_branch: "Janakpuri"`.

2. **Smart Branch Routing (n8n Switch Node):**
   - n8n mein lead update hote waqt ek `Switch` node lagta hai jo `preferred_branch` check karta hai:
     - **Path 1 (Kalu Sarai):** Alert seedha Kalu Sarai branch ke counsellor group/phone par jayega.
     - **Path 2 (Janakpuri):** Alert seedha Janakpuri branch ke counsellor group/phone par jayega.
     - **Path 3 (Noida):** Alert seedha Noida branch ke counsellor group/phone par jayega.
     - **Path 4 (Not Specified):** Alert Central Head Office ko jayega.

3. **Google Sheet Multi-Tab Filter:**
   - Sheet mein `Branch` column ke hisab se alag-alag tabs ban jaate hain taaki har branch ke counsellor ko sirf **apne centre ki leads** dikhein. Ek doosre ki leads mix nahi hoti!

### Client ko Pitch Karte Waqt Bada Fayda:

## 🤝 STEP 4: The 30-Minute Discovery & Closing Meeting Blueprint

Ye meeting client ko technical features dikhane ke liye nahi, balki unhe ye ehsaas karwane ke liye hai ki **har mahine unke lakhon rupaye delayed replies ki wajah se waste ho rahe hain**.

> *"Namaste [Sir/Ma'am]! Time nikaalne ke liye shukriya. Call ko structured aur productive rakhne ke liye agle 25-30 minute mein hum 3 cheezein karenge:*  
> 1. *Pehle 10 minute: Main aapke institute ke current admission enquiry aur follow-up process ko samjhunga.*  
> 2. *Agle 10 minute: Main aapko live WhatsApp AI system ka demo dikhaunga jo humne design kiya hai.*  
> 3. *Last 5-10 minute: Agar aapko lagta hai ki ye aapke institute ke admission conversions badha sakta hai, toh hum onboarding aur pricing discuss karenge. Agar aapko relevant na lage, toh hum wahin call wrap-up kar sakte hain.*  
> *Does that sound fair to you?"*

*(Isse client ko lagta hai ki aap ek professional consultant ho, koi desperate salesman nahi).*

### Stage 2: Deep Diagnostic Discovery & ROI Math (10 Minutes)
Pehle kabhi solution mat phenko. Pehle unka dard bahar nikaalo:

#### A. Situation & Pain Questions:

4. *"Counsellors ka kitna time same repetitive sawalon (fees structure, batch timing, location) ka jawab dene mein jata hai?"*

## 📢 8. Bulk Broadcast & Campaign Engine (v2 / Pilot Upsell)

Ek baar jab core Admission Bot deliver ho jaye, tab coaching institutes ko **Batch Launches, Festival Discounts, aur Scholarship Tests** ke liye Bulk Broadcast Engine upsell karo:

### A. Broadcast Execution Workflow
1. **Template Approval:** Meta se pre-approved marketing template banwayein (e.g. *"JEE New Batch starting 15th Nov, 20% Early Bird Scholarship Off"*).
2. **Audience Segmentation:** Supabase / Google Sheet se specific students filter karein (e.g. sirf `Class 11th`, `JEE`, ya `WARM/COLD leads`).
3. **Outbound Trigger:** ChakraHQ / n8n se bulk campaign schedule karke targeted batch blast karein.
4. **Automated Inbound Handling (The Killer Edge):** Jaise hi student broadcast par reply karta hai (e.g. *"Interested"* ya *"Fee kitni hai?"*), hamara **AI Agent automatically resume hokar student se conversation shuru kar deta hai** aur lead qualify karta hai! Counsellor ko ungli bhi nahi hilani padti.

### B. Marketing Message Cost Structure (Meta Rates + 18% GST)
| Recipients Count | Meta Marketing Cost (~₹0.86/msg) | Total Cost (Incl. 18% GST) |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **1,000 Students** | ₹860 | **~₹1,015** |
| **5,000 Students** | ₹4,310 | **~₹5,085** |
| **10,000 Students** | ₹8,630 | **~₹10,180** |

### C. Client Monetization Model (Aapka Revenue)
- **Setup Charge:** ₹500 – ₹1,500 per campaign (List segmentation, copy writing, Meta template submission).
- **Per Message Margin:** Meta rate + ₹0.20 – ₹0.40 margin per message.
- **Monthly Package:** ₹2,000 – ₹4,000/mo extra for up to 2 campaigns per month.
- **Advance Recharge:** Client se message charges pehle se **Wallet Top-up** mein lein (apne pocket se kabhi Meta bills mat bharo).

### D. Compliance & Anti-Ban Best Practices
- **Opt-in Mandate:** Sirf un students ko message bhejo jinhone pehle institute se enquire kiya ho, random bought lists par blast mat karo (number quality rating drop ho jayegi).
- **Frequency Capping:** Mahine mein max 2 se 4 bulk campaigns chalaayein.
- **Opt-out Handling:** Template ke niche button rakhein: *"Type STOP to unsubscribe"* — agar koi STOP bole toh Supabase mein `is_unsubscribed = true` mark karo.
- **Segmented Messaging:** HOT leads ko direct *"Demo Class Offer"*, COLD leads ko *"Course Brochure & Syllabus Overview"*.
