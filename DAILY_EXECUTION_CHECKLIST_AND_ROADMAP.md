### ⏱️ Phase 2: Unki Situation Samajhna & Problem ko Paise Mein Badalna (Minute 2–8)
*(Yahan client khud apna nuksan calculate karega — aapko bolne ki zarurat nahi padegi!)*

Unse yeh 3 sawal step-by-step pucho:

1. **Daily Enquiry Volume:**  
   > *"Sir, abhi jo aapke ads chal rahe hain, roz lagbhag kitni WhatsApp enquiries aati hain?"*  
   *(Client bolega: "Lagbhag 15 se 20 enquiries roz aati hain.")*

2. **The Delayed Response Drop:**  
   > *"Sir, jab shaam ko 8 PM ke baad ya Sunday ko enquiry aati hai, aur telecaller agle din 12 baje call karta hai — toh aapke experience ke hisaab se unme se lagbhag kitni leads bolti hain ki 'Maine nahi kiya / Dusra flat dekh liya'?"*  
   *(Client bolega: "Kam se kam 30% se 40% leads toh aise hi drop ho jaati hain.")*

3. **💥 The "Shock Math" Calculation (Client se khud multiply karwao):**  
   > *"Sir, aaiye ek simple math lagate hain:*  
   > • *Roz ki 20 enquiries mein se agar 30% late reply ki wajah se waste ho rahi hain, toh roz ki **6 leads waste** ho rahi hain.*  
   > • *Mahine mein 30 din x 6 leads = **180 high-intent buyers har mahine aapke haath se nikal rahe hain!***  
   >  
   > *Sir, aap mujhe batayein — agar in 180 leads mein se sirf **2% buyers (sirf 3 se 4 log)** bhi flat ya commercial unit book kar lete, **toh aapki per booking kitni brokerage ya profit banti?**"*

👉 **Client khud calculate karke bolega:**  
> *"Ek deal par kam se kam ₹1.5 Lakh se ₹3 Lakh banta hai... yani 3 deals ka matlab **₹4.5 Lakh se ₹9 Lakh ka nuksan**!"*

🔥 **Aapka Closing Strike:**  
> *"Exactly Sir! Yani har mahine aapka ad budget lagne ke baad bhi lagbhag ₹5 Lakh se ₹8 Lakh ka revenue sirf delayed response ki wajah se naali mein beh raha hai. Ab dekhiye hamara system is nuksan ko kaise zero karta hai."*

### 🔑 The Credentials Rule: Kab, Kya, Aur Kaise Maangna Hai?

> **⚠️ Golden Agency Rule:** **Meeting ke dauran passwords/credentials KABHI NAHI maangte!**  
> Agar aap sales pitch ke dauran Meta ID, Facebook login, ya OTP mangoge, toh client confuse aur insecure ho jayega aur bolega: *"Main apne digital marketer/IT wale se puch kar batata hoon"*, aur deal wahi atak jayegi.  
> **Meeting me sirf Brochure maango. Asli setup credentials hamesha ₹9,000 ADVANCE PAYMENT aane ke baad liye jate hain!**

#### 📦 Client Se Sirf Ye 5 Cheezein Chahiye Hoti Hain:

1. **Project Brochure & Price Sheet (PDF):**  
   - AI ko train karne ke liye project ke floor plans, pricing, amenities, possession date ki PDF ya website link.
2. **Dedicated WhatsApp Phone Number:**  
   - Ek phone number (SIM card) jisme normal WhatsApp app active na ho (ya fresh SIM). Ispe ek baar OTP aayega jab Meta Cloud API se link karenge.
3. **Meta Business Manager (WABA) Access:**  
   - *No Passwords needed!* Client ke saath ek **15-minute ka Google Meet** rakhte hain. Client screen share karega, aur hum unke Meta Business Suite me WhatsApp Cloud API app setup karwa ke webhook link paste karwa denge.
4. **CRM Email (Gmail):**  
   - Client ka password nahi chahiye! Hum apne Google Drive me unke project ka **Master CRM Sheet** banayenge aur client ke email ko "Editor" access de denge.
5. **Sales Team Mobile Numbers:**  
   - Unke 2-3 sales executives ke phone numbers jinhe Telegram Siren Alert group me add karna hai.
